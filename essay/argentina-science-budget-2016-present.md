# Argentina's science budget, 2016–present

This recent-period companion uses the same Grupo EPC–ANPRES and CIICTI source series as the long-range chart.

## Measure
National Budget Function for Science and Technology (Función Ciencia y Técnica, FCyT) as a percentage of GDP. It is not the same as total national R&D expenditure or CONICET's individual budget.

## Important limitation
The source material provides historical markers rather than a complete annual table. This graph therefore shows the recent observations available for 2019, 2023, 2024, 2025, and a 2026 projection. No observations are plotted for 2016–2018 or 2020–2022; the connecting lines are visual guides, not measured annual values.

The 2026 estimate of 0.164% is a projection based on budget execution through September 2026, not a final audited full-year result.
